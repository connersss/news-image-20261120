A program that draws one image summing up the world's news of Friday 20 November 2026: the historic Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit in China, the ICJ ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya, Cyclone Akvan bearing down on India's east coast, Anthropic's blockbuster stock-market debut, Ukraine's third strike on the Crimean Bridge in a week, and the migrant boat sinking off Senegal. It renders a newspaper front page — masthead with the date, six story cards with hand-drawn icons, headlines and summaries — using a small rasteriser and PNG encoder written from scratch in TypeScript, with zero dependencies and no network.
